<p><strong><span style="font-size:24px">Requires the library mod&nbsp;<a style="font-size:24px" href="https://curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/collective" target="_blank" rel="nofollow">Collective</a>.<br></span></strong></p>
<p><span style="font-size:18px">Void Vanguards is a sci-fi adventure mod made for the CurseForge 2025 ModJam. It throws the player into an ancient conflict between The Vanguard and The Voidborn, two opposing forces locked in a war that stretches across dimensions. You&rsquo;ll follow an immersive questline with advanced species and cosmic magic. The story is uncovered piece by piece as you progress.<br><br>A mysterious sky ship has appeared in orbit, and strange signals are being detected across the world. Maybe you should take a closer look through your <strong>Spyglass</strong>. Make sure you're prepared, as this could trigger an event with great risk and great value.<br><br>Travel through portals to the custom Cosmos dimension, where the Vanguard Station and the Voidborn Asteroid await. Find enemies and loot along the way! Decide which of the two quest paths you'd like to take.<br><br>Compatible with multiplayer servers! Each player has their own questline and their own structures.<br><br><span><span><iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/-muJghaPMPU?wmode=transparent" width="638" height="358" allowfullscreen="allowfullscreen"></iframe></span></span><br><span style="font-size:16px"><strong>Note:</strong> There is currently a known issue with <span style="text-decoration:underline;font-size:16px">Sodium</span> that renders the sky ship in front of other blocks. I wasn't able to fix this in time for the jam deadline.</span><br><br>You'll come across mysterious high-tech objects:<br><picture><img src="https://github.com/Serilum/.cdn/raw/main/projects/void-vanguards/d.png?=1" width="1127" height="644"><br><br>And well protected gates to different dimensions:<br><img src="https://github.com/Serilum/.cdn/raw/main/projects/void-vanguards/c.png"></picture><br><br><span style="font-size:18px"><span style="font-size:24px">The mod generates two structures inside the cosmos dimension. You'll get access to one of the two, depending on your choices, after the quest.</span><br><br><strong>The Vanguard headquarters:</strong></span><br><picture><img src="https://github.com/Serilum/.cdn/raw/main/projects/void-vanguards/a.png" alt=""></picture><br><br><span style="font-size:18px"><strong>The forwarding base of the Voidborn:</strong></span><br><strong><picture><img src="https://github.com/Serilum/.cdn/raw/main/projects/void-vanguards/b.png" alt="" width="1127" height="634"></picture><br><br></strong><span style="font-size:18px">Credits to:<br><strong>&nbsp;cryzz60</strong>, for<strong>&nbsp;</strong>the initial Vanguard Station schematic.<br>&nbsp;<strong>grimes2013</strong>,&nbsp;for the initial Voidborn Asteroid schematic.<br></span></span></p>
